Features

A screenshot tool that does the whole job.

Fast when you need one image. Flexible when the page needs more care.

↕

Full page, visible area, or selection

Save the whole scrolling page or viewport, or draw a rectangle or freeform lasso and capture it immediately.

◷

Timed and continuous capture

Take one delayed shot, repeat on a schedule, or watch a selected area for a limited number of runs.

✎

Annotate and protect

Add arrows, text, rectangles, blur, or pixelation, and hide sensitive details before export.

↓

Copy, save, and export

Each result opens ready to copy, zoom, annotate, download as PNG, or export as a paginated PDF.

The extension

Open Lumen and capture.

One click captures the whole page. More options are ready when you need them.

  • Rectangle, lasso, responsive, and timed capture options
  • A clean result page with copy, zoom, PNG, PDF, and edit actions
  • A local library for review, annotation, and export

Privacy

Your screenshots stay yours.

Lumen works locally by default and does not require an account. Privacy Shield turns on automatic redaction, review before save, and metadata minimization together.

Read the privacy policy →

Reliability

Made for real webpages.

Lumen handles long pages, sticky navigation, lazy-loaded content, and responsive layouts. Its live test matrix includes GitHub and MDN pages up to 41,184 pixels tall.
