Honest tool choice

Use Lumen when the screenshot matters tomorrow.

A system shortcut wins for a disposable snip. GoFullPage is established for one-click full-page capture and PDF. FireShot offers broad editing and export depth. Lumen’s edge is the connected local loop: capture, explain, repeat, compare, and return.

Tool Best fit What Lumen adds
Screenshot shortcut The fastest one-off visible-viewport snip Full scrollable pages, repeatable areas, annotation, context, and history

FireShot Broad editor, batch, and export-format depth A focused local capture-to-change-review loop with bounded monitors and duplicate suppression
