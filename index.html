Private by default. Tested on real pages.

Lumen is designed to be dependable without turning your screenshots into someone else’s data.

Your working library stays on your device.

There is no Lumen account to create. Privacy Shield can turn on review-before-save, redact sensitive details, keep output local, and pause unattended monitors. Turn it off whenever you want to return to your usual settings.
