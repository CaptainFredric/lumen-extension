Features

Fast when you need one image. Flexible when the page needs more care.

↕

Full-page capture

Save a long scrolling page in one click instead of stitching screenshots by hand.

⌖

Area and lasso

Draw a rectangle or a freeform lasso when you only need part of the page.

◷

Timed captures

Run a delayed shot, repeat on a schedule, or capture continuously for a set time.

Add arrows, text, rectangles, blur, or pixelation with undo and redo.

↓

PNG and PDF

Export a detailed image or a readable, paginated PDF that stays sharp when zoomed.

Local photo library

Keep captures organized and searchable without sending them to a Lumen account.

The extension

Open Lumen and capture.

The main button saves the full page immediately. The arrow—or a press and hold—opens the additional capture choices when you need them.

  • Full page, visible area, rectangle, or lasso
  • Desktop, tablet, and mobile captures in one run
  • Review, annotate, save, or export from the same extension

Privacy

Your screenshots stay yours.

Lumen works locally by default and does not require an account. Privacy Shield can review sensitive captures before they are saved or exported.
