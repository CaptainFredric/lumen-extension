Optional site access Checking Chrome access…

    Manual captures use the active tab. Tablet, mobile, and timed captures ask for site access only when needed. One-shot access is revoked after capture; active monitors retain their site until stopped.

    Google Drive Checking optional connection…

    Drive is optional, uses the narrow drive.file scope, and uploads only after you choose the reviewed-image action. Revoking it does not delete files already in Drive.
